Entries can carry claims: plain statements with a date, an origin and a status (active, in review, or archived with a reason). They are stored, synced and shown on the Documentation tab under Sources. The card's "Suggest a change" button is replaced by an inline "Know something more?" input at the end of every tab that sends feedback in place. A short note under the input tells the reader what happens to their note; the default is replaceable through the plugin slot system.
