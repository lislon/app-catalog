Entries can carry claims: plain statements with a date, an origin and an active/archived status (archived with a reason). They are stored, synced and shown on the Documentation tab under Sources. The card's "Suggest a change" button is replaced by an inline "Know something more?" input at the end of every tab that sends feedback in place.
